# Concept angles

An angle is the twist that makes an idea clickable. Same idea, different angle, different video. Give each shortlisted idea its best angle plus one alternative. Name the angle by slug in the Idea Record.

| Slug | Angle | Shape | Works for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| price-tiers | Price tiers | $1 vs $100 vs $1,000 version of the same thing | Food, gear, services, software | Real prices only; label estimates |
| time-tiers | Time tiers | 10 minutes vs 1 hour vs 24 hours | Skills, builds, cooking | Each tier must look visibly different |
| matchup | Matchup / drag race | Two things head to head on one fair test | Tools, methods, products | State the test before the result |
| tried-n-days | I tried X for N days | Rules, daily log, surprise, verdict | Habits, tools, routines | Needs the real days filmed |
| superlative | Superlative | The biggest, cheapest, fastest, worst | Places, products, attempts | The claim must be checkable |
| familiar-twist | Familiar but unexpected | A known format with one twist | Anything | The twist has to show in the title |
| access | Exclusive access | Somewhere or someone viewers can't reach | Kitchens, back offices, experts | Permission and privacy |
| expert-beginner | Expert vs beginner | Same task, two skill levels | Skills, tools | Don't mock the beginner |
| reverse | Reverse it | Do the common advice backwards and see | Advice-heavy niches | Must be safe to try |
| constraint | Impossible constraint | No budget, one tool, one hour | Builds, cooking, business | The constraint drives the drama |
| myth-test | Myth test | Common belief → real test → verdict | Advice niches | Fair test, shown on camera |
| cost-of | The real cost of | What X actually costs in money, time, mistakes | Business, food, DIY | Real numbers only |
| insider | Insider truth | What people in the trade know and don't say | Any trade | Opinions labeled as opinions |
| ranked | Ranked | Criteria first, bottom to top, surprise #1 | Lists | Criteria shown up front |

Add new angles when the creator's world suggests one; mark them `new`.

**Shorts / Reels.** Multi-part angles (price-tiers, time-tiers, ranked, tried-n-days) fit a Short only as a list of 3 or fewer (`catalog/short-shapes.md`), strongest first, no slow build to #1. Otherwise not at all. Single-point angles (myth-test, superlative, reverse, insider, familiar-twist) fit most shapes; each shape lists the angles that fit it.
