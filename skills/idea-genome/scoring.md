# Step 9. Idea scoring

## Explain first (once per session, before the first score is shown)

Say this in plain words, adapted to the creator:
- The score asks six questions about the idea, each rated 1–5 and weighted: Click appeal ×3, Proof ×3, Personality fit ×2, Freshness ×2, Effort ×1, Past results ×1. Max 60.
- 42+ = make it next. 30–41 = park it, and I'll say what would raise it. Under 30 = reject.
- A Short / Reel uses its own table (below): max 70, 49+ make it next, 35–48 park, under 35 reject.
- It rates the IDEA (is it worth making?), not how well the video will be made. Execution quality is checked later, in video-genome's reviews.
- Some lines can rise before or during production: Proof rises when the missing proof gets made; Click can rise with a sharper title or thumbnail. Effort and Personality fit usually don't move.
- Every line comes with one line of evidence. Where there's no data (a new channel has none for Click or Freshness), I mark it as a guess.
- You can overrule a park; the record notes it was your call.

## The six questions

| Criterion | Weight | 5 means | 1 means | Evidence to cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Click appeal | 3 | A stranger would click the best title on sight | Only existing fans care | Similar titles/patterns that broke out (sourced), or "guess" |
| Proof | 3 | The creator has the real thing to show today | Needs proof they don't have | Items in Proof we have / still need |
| Personality fit | 2 | Delivers the lead personality pick with no help (or through the brand's fallback, e.g. spatial motion, without forcing); only-you line holds | Needs a twist bolted on | Genome wording it matches, only-you line |
| Freshness | 2 | Nobody in the niche took this angle | Covered to death, same angle | Gap scan results (sourced), or "guess" |
| Effort | 1 | Makeable inside the publishing rhythm | Breaks the rhythm | What it needs: set pieces, shoots, tests |
| Past results | 1 | Similar formats/angles over-performed in `results-log.md` | Similar ones under-performed | Log rows; 3 and "no log yet" if none |

**Total** = sum of score × weight (max 60).
- **42+**: recommend making it next.
- **30–41**: park; say what would raise it and to how much.
- **Under 30**: reject, with the reason in the bank.

## How to show it

Score table, then one evidence line per criterion, then a **rises to N if…** line for any criterion that can realistically move (e.g. "Proof 3 → 4 (total 44) once the tool is actually used in the video"). End with the verdict. If the creator overrules a park or reject, write "Creator's call: <their words>" under the verdict.

Write every score into `idea-bank.md`. Rescore after development if the brainstorm gave an early score; keep the earlier number in the evidence line ("Fresh 4 (was 3): …").

Any idea that breaks a truth rule is rejected before scoring, whatever its appeal. Other Genome conflicts are ruled in Step 7 first, not scored away.

## A Short / Reel

Shorts get swiped, not clicked, and they spread by being sent. So Click appeal is replaced by Stop + hold, and Send reason is added. The rest stay.

**Before scoring:** a Short that fails the stranger test (`short-form.md` S2) is rejected, like a truth-rule break. It never gets a number.

| Criterion | Weight | 5 means | 1 means | Evidence to cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stop + hold | 3 | A stranger watches the first 2 s and stays to the end | Swiped in the first second | The hook's three layers; shape examples that held (sourced), or "guess" |
| Send reason | 2 | A clear person would send it to a clear someone | Nobody would forward it | The send-reason line |
| Proof | 3 | The real thing is on screen | Needs proof they don't have | Proof items. Long-video footage counts only if the moment passes the stranger test |
| Personality fit | 2 | As the long table | As the long table | Genome wording, only-you line |
| Freshness | 2 | Nobody in the niche did this shape on this point | Same Short everywhere | Example search (sourced), or "guess" |
| Effort | 1 | The native vertical re-shoot fits the publishing rhythm | The re-shoot breaks the rhythm | What the re-shoot needs. Count the fresh vertical recording, never "the footage exists, just cut it" |
| Past results | 1 | Similar Shorts over-performed in `results-log.md` | Similar ones under-performed | Log rows; 3 and "no log yet" if none |

**Total** = sum of score × weight (max 70). Same cut points as long videos, scaled (70% and 50%):
- **49+**: recommend making it next.
- **35–48**: park; say what would raise it and to how much.
- **Under 35**: reject, with the reason in the bank.

The bank's score column takes `NN/70` for Shorts.
