# Step 3 for a still carousel (replaces develop.md 3b and Hooks)

Purpose: turn one idea into one worked-through carousel, with the creator. Craft rules live in script-genome `catalog/carousel.md` (load it if script-genome is installed; otherwise use the lines below). Answers go under `## Develop rounds`, in the creator's words.

A carousel is its own post, never a screenshot of a video. It is not a discovery format: it earns saves and sends from followers. One idea per carousel, one record per carousel.

From develop.md, still run: 3a research (examples of carousels on the point, with links), 3e versions to test, 3f only-you line.

## C1. Source and stranger test
Ask which (plain setup per option): **written for the page (default)** or **cut from a script or long video** (script-genome carousel.md §11: re-hook, drop beats that need voice or face). Then pass or fail: slide 1 read alone, out of order, no caption. Fail = reject, in the bank with the reason.

## C2. Message and who it is sent to
One sentence, 12 words or fewer. Then the type of person who would send it, in plain words (not a name).

## C3. Order
Pain-first, take-first or payoff-first (a checklist). Offer two with a two-line example each in the creator's topic. Recommend pain-first for a take idea, payoff-first for a checklist. Say it is a test, not a rule.

## C4. Slide plan
One line per slide: job, claim, what leaves the next slide open (the table in the record). Slide 1 and slide 2 each written to stand alone. A keeper slide the creator would screenshot. Show it as a table on a decision page or in chat; the creator approves the plan before any words are final.

## C5. The one ask
Read the growth plan's `cta_allowed_now` and `house_bans`; no plan: stage "no audience yet" (an implied send line, or save if the keeper is a checklist). Write the exact ask and say which stage allows it. One ask only.

## C6. Look and caption
Copy the brand's carousel look field (brand-genome `vertical.md`). Missing: write `look: owed` and tell the creator brand-genome Stage 8 §8 fills it from what they post. Draft the caption: it adds a thought, never retells, never claims something not yet true.

Then run checks (Steps 7 and 8), the carousel score (`scoring.md`) and write the carousel record variant.
