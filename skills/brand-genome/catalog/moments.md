# Catalog: big moments, break-from-reality moments

**Tier:** **C** common · **R** rare · **F** funky. Shown as storyboard strips or short click-to-play animatics, with a benchmark person as the subject.

## Break-from-reality moments (Stage 4, own page; the bank is written right after)

A short scene where the video steps outside reality for a laugh or a point. Never pre-generated: each video's plan proposes 2–3 spots tied to its beats, and final videos keep 1–2. They count as "big moments only", so they're allowed to fail. Offer ~10 on the moments-library page; it is a library, so the creator keeps every one they like.

| Slug | Name | Tier | What happens | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| department-of-me | The Department of Me | R | Clones of the creator play the roles the video needs (researcher, critic, accountant, intern) and argue, hand off work or file complaints | Processes with several steps or opinions |
| tiny-me | Tiny Me | R | A miniature creator inside whatever is on screen: walking a timeline, hiding behind a chart bar | Screen demos, zooming into a detail |
| nature-doc | Nature Documentary | C | A hushed narrator observes the creator like a wild animal | Routines, failures, struggle montages |
| the-standup | The Standup | R | The video's things (tools, ingredients, files) report status, blockers and excuses | Recaps, blame for what went wrong |
| trailer | The Trailer | C | A mundane task cut like a movie trailer: booms, title cards, a release date | Launches, big efforts |
| infomercial | The Infomercial | C | Late-night infomercial: "Are YOU tired of…", black-and-white fails, a phone number | Problems and fixes |
| sports-commentary | Sports Commentary | R | Two commentators call the action play by play | Fast processes, competitions |

Each kept template is written as: name, what happens, fits, inputs needed (roles, cast list, script), and the cheapest way to draft it (paper cutout animatic, still frames, generated stills, stock-free synthesized sound).
