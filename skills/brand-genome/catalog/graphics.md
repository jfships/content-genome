## Backgrounds (Stage 4.8, backdrops: behind a talking person or avatar, and behind title cards)

Three sections on the page. **Every tile must look like the real thing as it would appear behind a person** (cover the name: could you guess it?). Scenes are the photos in `../benchmark/backdrops/<slug>.jpg` (made once, shipped with the skill, no people, no logos, no real seals); plains and patterns are drawn in the palette.
