# Instagram carousels and Stories: what the rules rest on

Read by Stage 8 §8. Checked 2026-10-17. Re-check before relying on any number; Instagram changes these.

## How sure each rule is

| Rule | Basis |
|---|---|
| Stories reach people who already follow you; growth comes from Reels | Instagram's own ranking post (firm) |
| Up to 20 slides per carousel; 4:5 (1080×1350), 1:1, 1.91:1 allowed | Platform spec (firm) |
| Under about 300 followers, skip Stories | Practitioner guidance (soft) |
| 6–10 slides, 7–10 best | Vendor guides, no study named (soft) |
| Slide 1 is the hook, swipe cue on slide 1 only, progress mark on value slides | Vendor guides (soft) |
| Under about 25 words per value slide, two type sizes at most | Vendor guides (soft) |
| One action on the last slide; links are slightly de-weighted | Vendor guides (soft) |
| Carousel text kept out of the top 120 px and bottom 150 px; logo under 15% of the slide | Vendor guides; the 120/150 px are house numbers |
| Text sizes (80 px headline, 44 px body at 1080 wide) | House numbers, no source gives any |
| Profile grid crop | Sources disagree (centered 1:1 vs 3:4); check live |
| Stories safe zones: top 250 px, bottom 340 px, 65 px sides | Third-party readings of Meta's diagram |

Vendor performance figures (saves "35% more", 60–75% slide 1→2 swipe, completion 25–40%) are cited to unnamed studies. Don't quote them to a creator as fact.

## Stories rules in full

1080×1920, H.264 MP4 or JPG/PNG under 30 MB. Video frames up to 60 s; aim 5–15 s. Stills show about 5–7 s. First second decides: face or motion at once. 3–4 frames: context → value → one action. Native and loose, not an ad; designed flyers get skipped. One idea per frame, readable in 2 s, large type with a shadow or solid block. Always caption speech. Exact brand colors and fonts. One sticker per frame, on the last frame, inside the safe zone. Poll is the easiest interaction. Link sticker: custom text with a specific promise, one link frame per sequence, last, with tracking tags (link labels that count clicks). 2–7 Stories a day at most. Highlights: evergreen only; cover is one centered icon or word in the brand colors.

Launch-day recipe for a new long video (few followers): hook clip 5–10 s (face + the most surprising moment) → proof frame (raw screen or before/after, native text) → poll → link frame (vertical crop of the thumbnail or the creator pointing, link sticker with custom text). Same day, post a Reel cut; the Reel reaches new people.

Don't: Stories under the follower gate, daily filler, Reel reposts as the only content, a 16:9 thumbnail pasted on a color field.
