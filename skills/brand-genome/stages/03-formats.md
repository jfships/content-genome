# Stage 3: Formats (the shows they'll repeat)

**This stage decides formats, not content.** A format is a recipe a viewer recognizes: same structure, new content each time. Topics and episode ideas are the creator's; they bring them, or brainstorm them in idea-genome, one video at a time. Nothing here proposes what a video should be about.

**No voting pages.** Formats are judged with words, so this is one question in chat (AskUserQuestion). Internal words like "pillar", "reach" or "funnel" never reach the creator.

## Propose 5–6 formats

Start from `../catalog/vertical-voice-formats.md` (format recipes), the research playbook (which formats drive the best videos in their niche, and what nobody there is doing), and the interview (their proof, story beats and how far they'll go). Write each in plain words, one line with a made-up example that fits their world:

> **The 60-second fix:** one common problem, solved on camera, with a before and after. *Example: a dog that hates the dryer.*

The example shows the shape; it is not a suggested episode. Say so if they ask.

## Ask

Say what it changes: "A format is a show you repeat. It decides the shape of your videos, their length, and the piece viewers come back for. Pick the ones you'd make again and again." Multi-select; recommend one long-form and one vertical format as the starting pair. Keep 2–3.

## Write each kept format into `formats.md`

- **Name** (it can become the series name in the series tag, Stage 7).
- **Promise:** what the viewer gets, in one line.
- **Structure:** 4–7 beats in order (hook, setup, the turn, the payoff…).
- **Length:** long and/or vertical, and target duration.
- **The recurring piece:** a segment, a signature moment, a running gag or a catchphrase that makes it recognizable.
- **Personality check:** how it delivers the lead personality pick. A format that is only "informational" when "entertaining" leads needs a twist built in.
- **Effort:** low / medium / high to make, so they can keep a steady cadence.

Show the kept formats back as a short list and ask if anything's missing.

Story arcs, hooks and script frameworks are chosen per video in video-genome; don't lock them here.

## Output

`formats.md`: the kept formats with structure and recurring piece, and the recommended starting pair.
