# Stage 6: Sound palette

Every page here is built from `../page-template/` with the words in `../voting-pages.md`. Open the stage with its plain hand-off (what it is, two pages, about 5 minutes, nothing happens without their say, they can stop anytime). This stage is free; sound tools come up in Stage 7 only.

Sound is half of how a video feels and the part creators think about least. This stage decides what the brand sounds like, so every video doesn't start from "find some music".

Both steps are **libraries**: keep every kind of music and every sound they like; each video picks from them.

## 6a. Music (library)

- `music.json`: 12–16 lanes from `../catalog/sound.md`, across tiers, each with mood in 3 words, genre, tempo range (BPM), instruments, and its line "For: …" (under talking, a reveal, an ending…).
- Each card plays a short Web Audio sketch (drums, bass and chords at the lane's tempo) with one big play/pause. The page says plainly they are sketches of tempo and energy, not finished music: "Your library saves the recipe; real tracks are made from it when a video is edited." Keep in library / Discard; keep as many as they like.
- **Phrase length:** with tempo now set, write how many beats a scene change spans (8 for long, 4 for vertical, from the lane's tempo) into the motion table's Phrase length row. No question.
- **Rules to write down:** no vocals under speech; the lane changes or drops at turning points; silence before a big line is a tool.
- **Licensing:** only generated music or music with a license that allows the platform. Never commercial songs.

## 6b. Sound effects (library, skippable)

- Every sound has a named role: whoosh (wipes), tick (counters, typing), hit (big type lands), click (UI, cursor), riser (before a reveal), drop or stop (punchline), plus 2–4 that are theirs, from the taste answers (paper, workshop, kitchen, retro game…).
- Role options in `../catalog/sound.md`. 2–4 options per moment kind (synthesized in Web Audio), one page with a section per moment kind, each sound playing **with the visual it belongs to**. Keep in library / Discard; several per kind is fine. Build it like `music.json` (sound in `head`, one play per card). Skipped → none.
- Rule to write down: sound effects on transitions and reveals, not every cut. A starting cap of about 1 per 3 s.

The sting and sound logo are made in Stage 7, because they can only be judged with their picture.

## Output

`sound.md` (the music lanes with BPM ranges and prompts, SFX roles with file names, rules, the pages' artifact URLs) and the chosen SFX files in `01 Brand Kit/sound/`, each with a `Type: kit` row in `07 Asset Library/asset-index.md`.

**Hand-off at the end of Sound** (one line): "Sound is done: your music and sound effects are saved. Next: your brand kit, the logo, name tag, intro and other pieces with your name on them (9 steps)."
