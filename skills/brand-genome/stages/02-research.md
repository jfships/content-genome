# Stage 2: Channel research → playbook

Run three research passes in parallel (four if a Short platform was picked) (subagents with web search). Cap: 5 minutes. Say "Researching your niche, about five minutes" first; nothing else is asked while it runs. Every finding is dated and cited.

1. **Hooks, retention and packaging.** The first 30 s, re-hooks, length, packaging-first titles and thumbnails, A/B testing, Shorts/Reels rules, real face vs avatar, and each platform's current disclosure rules for synthetic media.
2. **Niche benchmarks.** 8–12 channels in the creator's space: format, length, cadence, what their breakout videos share, how they use free stuff, crowded vs open lanes, and realistic growth expectations.
3. **Formats in the niche.** Which repeatable formats (named series, recurring segments, challenge structures) the benchmark channels use, which ones drive their best videos, and what nobody in the niche is doing. This feeds Stage 3.
4. **Short-form benchmarks** (only if Shorts, Reels or TikTok were picked). About 5 short-form creators in the niche, 3 Shorts each: the first frame (what's on screen, the hook text, the first line), the layout, the captions (chunk size, position, color), how it ends, the length, and which of the six Short shapes it is (`../catalog/vertical-voice-formats.md`). Keep links to real Shorts: video-genome shows them as examples before a Short's shape is locked. This feeds Stages 3 and 8.

Write `02 Research/channel-playbook.md`: defaults to follow and to break on purpose, with sources and a caveat on vendor-published data. Present the 2–3 decisions it raises, one at a time, each starting with what the answer changes.

The playbook guides **how** ideas get made (hooks, length, cadence, Shorts mix, open lanes); it never supplies the ideas. Those are the creator's (or come from idea-genome, one Idea Record per video). Its 5 most important defaults, the benchmark channels and the short-form benchmarks go into the Brand Genome as part of who the creator is.

In demo mode, the persona's niche is researched for real. The findings are real even if the creator isn't.
