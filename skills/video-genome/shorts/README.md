# Shorts track: Shorts, Reels, TikTok

A Short is its own video, not a small episode and never a cut of the long edit. It runs on this track: its own checklist, pacing, checks and review. The long-form files (`plan/run-sheet.md`, `paths/`, `review/checks.md` gates 1 and 4) don't apply to a Short except where this folder points to them.

**Use this track when** the output is a Short, Reel or TikTok: a new one, one made from a long video's material, or a batch. A batch is several Shorts, each run through this track on its own (one Idea Record, one plan, one review per Short).

**Two rules that never bend**
- **A crop of the long edit is never a draft.** Long-video footage is used only as parts (a reaction, a screen, a clip), rebuilt in a tall frame with a hook recorded fresh for vertical.
- **The long video's order never transfers.** A Short is rebuilt in one shape (`shapes.md`), not kept in the episode's order.

## Read
- `shapes.md`: the six Short shapes.
- `build.md`: shooting, the tall rough draft, pace, layout, captions, cutting.
- `checks.md`: the Short's gates, the timing numbers and the scored review.
- `platforms.md`: files, covers, endings and music rules per platform, and the results to log.
- From the main skill: `decision-pages.md`, `craft/layout.md` (9:16 rules), `craft/motion.md`, `craft/sound.md`, `setup/gotchas.md`.

## Where it lives
- A Short made from an episode: `03 Videos/EP NN <slug>/shorts/S<n> <slug>/`.
- A standalone Short: `03 Videos/SH NN <slug>/` (numbers continue from the highest `SH NN`).
- Each Short folder has its own `idea.md`, `plan.md`, `debt.md`, `log.md`, `work/`, `_review/`, `out/`.

## Flow

Tracker line on every reply that moves the work: "Short 2 of 3 · plan · decision 3 of 8". Steps 0a–0b (brand, tools) are the main skill's.

| Step | What | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| S0. About | One confirmed line: "This Short is about ___, for ___, on ___ (platforms). Its message: ___." | Line confirmed; Idea Record present (idea-genome's Short record, or one written here with the same fields) |
| S1. Plan | The Short checklist below, one decision at a time | Short gate 1 passes (`checks.md`) |
| S2. Asset debt | `plan/asset-debt.md`, same as long-form | Everything owed listed, library searched |
| S3. Tall rough draft | `build.md` "Rough draft": built 9:16 from the start, temp voice or the creator's existing words, before anything is recorded | Creator watched it and said what changes; switch-model nudge given (main SKILL.md, "Which model for which part") |
| S4. Record | Hook and lines recorded vertical (`build.md` "Recording") | Takes in, transcribed |
| S5. Build | Cut, pace, lay out, caption, sound (`build.md`) | Short gates 2–3 pass |
| S6. Review | Timing numbers + scored review + fresh reviewer (`checks.md`) | Every score 8+ |
| S7. Deliver | One file per platform (`platforms.md`) | Each file meets its platform's spec |
| S8. Results | `platforms.md` "Results" | 48 h and 28 d numbers logged |

Every session ends with `review/session-close.md`, as in the main skill.

## The Short checklist (S1)

Fill these in `plan.md`. Items taken from the Idea Record are confirmed in one pass (`plan/intake.md`, "Confirm in one pass"); open items are decided one at a time, words in chat, looks on a page.

1. **Source.** Written for vertical (default): re-scripted, hook recorded fresh. Or made from a long video's material: only moments that pass the stranger test, still rebuilt tall with a fresh hook. Never "the long edit, cropped".
2. **Message.** One sentence, 12 words or fewer: what a stranger walks away with. If it needs the long video to make sense, the Short fails the stranger test and is rejected, not rescued.
3. **Shape.** Exactly one from `shapes.md`. Before it's locked, show the creator 3 real Shorts of that shape in their niche (links found by search, never invented).
4. **Hook.** Written word for word, on three layers that land together in the first 1–2 s: the first frame (the creator on screen, or their real material; never solely a generated figure), the spoken line, and 3–7 words of hook text on screen from frame 1. Main and Backup. Frame 1 must make sense with no context. No greeting, intro, logo, sting, sound logo or name tag before it.
5. **Length and word budget.** Shorts 20–45 s, Reels 20–60 s, TikTok 25–45 s (61–75 s only for creators in TikTok's Creator Rewards). The shortest length that lands the idea. Word budget at about 150 words a minute: ~37 words per 15 s, ~75 per 30 s, ~110 per 45 s, ~150 per 60 s. The script must fit; footage may run over and gets cut after watching.
6. **Script.** The shape's beats, in order, with the time each lands. Call-and-response: 4 exchanges or fewer, each one escalating. The first payoff (laugh, twist or result) within 3 s.
7. **Last line.** Written word for word: a payoff, a button line, a deadpan hold of 1 s or less, or a loop (the last line runs into the first, visually and in words). No outro, no "thanks for watching", no subscribe ask, no end card, no sting, nothing that announces the end.
8. **Platform goal and ending.** Per platform: YouTube Shorts names the long video for the Related video link; Instagram builds reach on its own (the send reason below matters most there), with a call to action only as on-screen text over the last line; TikTok's sound policy (`platforms.md`).
9. **Send reason.** Who would send this to someone, and why, in one line.
10. **Layout and look.** One layout from the brand's vertical layouts (`build.md` "Layout"), the brand's caption and hook-text styles, the tall backdrop. Summarized in chat; a page only when two options are both good.
11. **Music and sound.** The brand's vertical lane, voice-led (`platforms.md` for music rights).
12. **Truth and disclosure.** Same rules as long-form.

Short gate 1 (`checks.md`) runs before asking for approval.
