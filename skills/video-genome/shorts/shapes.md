# Short shapes

The same six shapes as idea-genome's `catalog/short-shapes.md` (same names and slugs; keep the two in step). Every Short names exactly one in `plan.md`; when the Idea Record already names it, it's taken from there.

**The long video's order never transfers.** A Short made from a long video is rebuilt in one of these shapes, not kept in the episode's order.

Before a shape is locked, show the creator 3 real example Shorts of it in their niche (`README.md`, checklist item 3). Examples below are made up and labeled as examples, in a neutral niche (home cooking).

## 1. Payoff first → loop (`hook-payoff-loop`)

- **Beats (2–4):** the claim or result in 1–2 s → one supporting beat → last line runs into the first.
- **Length:** 15–35 s.
- **Example:** "This $4 pan beats my $200 one." → the egg slides off → "...and that's why this $4 pan beats my $200 one."
- **Watch out:** the loop must read as one sentence; a forced loop feels like a glitch.
- **Angles that fit:** superlative, cost-of, familiar-twist, constraint.

## 2. Myth-bust (`myth-bust`)

- **Beats (3–4):** the hyped claim (said, shown, or a clip) → flat reaction → the one fact that kills it → button.
- **Length:** 20–40 s.
- **Example:** "Searing seals in the juices." → blank stare → weigh two steaks after resting → "Juices: not sealed."
- **Watch out:** one fact only, and it must be real; the clip of the claim stays short.
- **Angles that fit:** myth-test, insider, reverse.

## 3. List of 3 or fewer (`list-of-3`)

- **Beats:** strongest item first, or a count that pulls → up to 3 items, no filler.
- **Length:** 25–45 s; 3 items max under 45 s.
- **Example:** "3 knives you don't need. Number one is in every starter set."
- **Watch out:** no slow countdown to a surprise #1; each item stands alone in one beat.
- **Angles that fit:** ranked, price-tiers, time-tiers (only as 3 quick items).

## 4. Before / after (`before-after`)

- **Beats:** the result in frame 1 → rewind to how.
- **Length:** 15–40 s.
- **Example:** a glossy sourdough loaf on screen → "Three weeks ago it looked like this." → the flat first try → the one change.
- **Watch out:** the "after" must make sense with no context; the "how" is one change, not a tutorial.
- **Angles that fit:** tried-n-days, expert-beginner, constraint.

## 5. Skit / POV (`skit-pov`)

- **Beats:** the creator plays both sides (e.g. the hype guy and himself), deadpan; 4 exchanges or fewer, each one escalates.
- **Length:** 15–45 s.
- **Example:** POV: the recipe blogger's life story vs you scrolling for the ingredients.
- **Watch out:** frame 1 must show the setup without a caption explaining it; stop at the biggest exchange.
- **Angles that fit:** familiar-twist, insider, reverse.

## 6. Reaction / reply (`reaction-reply`)

- **Beats:** the clip, comment or other Short being answered (reply-with-a-Short, remix) → the creator's take → button.
- **Length:** 15–40 s. The reacted-to clip is a third of the runtime or less. The creator's face is on screen in frame 1.
- **Example:** a comment "You can't cook rice in a kettle" on screen → creator holding a kettle → it works, or it doesn't.
- **Watch out:** the take is the content, not the clip; check the rights to the clip.
- **Angles that fit:** myth-test, matchup, insider.
