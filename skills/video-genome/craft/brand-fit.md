# Brand fit: when the brand's graphics don't fit a moment

A brand file (Genome, Brand Sheet or included kit) can't foresee every moment. When nothing it kept fits what a moment needs, propose your own, inside fixed limits. Never force a kept move onto a moment it doesn't fit, and never quietly drift off-brand.

## Locked (never changed)
- **Colors:** only the main pack's colors (and the neutrals `brand_check.py` ignores). Tints and opacity of those colors are fine. A backup kit lends moves, motion and layouts, never its colors or fonts.
- **Fonts:** only the pack's fonts, in the jobs the brand gives them.
- **House rules:** every one, including banned words and things they never want.
- **Brand kit pieces:** sting, sound logo, wordmark, series tag, name tag and end card are used exactly as made.
- **Truth rules.**

## Open (may be new)
- Shapes and graphic devices (a stamp, a map, a chart style, a frame, a texture within the palette).
- Moves and entrances, built to the brand's motion feel (eases and timings from its motion table; `craft/motion.md`).
- Layouts and placement (`craft/layout.md`).
- Transitions that aren't reserved for a kit piece.
- Break-from-reality treatments.

## How it's shown
- Every off-brand option carries `outside: true` on its decision page ("Outside your brand") and is never the only option: at least one brand option sits beside it, unless none fits, which the page's `what` line says.
- It can be "Our pick" when it's genuinely better; the `why` line says what the brand's options lacked.
- In chat, one line: "Your brand has no way to show a map; here's one in your colors and fonts."
- Log each one used in the episode's `log.md` under **Outside the brand**: what, where (time + line), why.

## Afterwards
Everything logged under **Outside the brand** goes to the brand review at session close (`review/session-close.md`).
