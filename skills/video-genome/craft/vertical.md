# Vertical: Shorts, Reels, TikTok

Vertical is not the long video cropped. Follow the brand's vertical rules (layouts per format, caption style, tag and cover rules, the first-3-seconds rule, vertical music lane, safe zones). This file is how to cut and build them.

## Picking moments

- From the plan's vertical list: 2–4 moments that stand alone without the long video.
- Each needs a hook in the first 3 s: a visual hook, a spoken hook and a text hook together. No logo first.
- Length: 11–45 s is a safe range; check each platform's current limits.

## Building

- **Re-lay out each moment in 9:16 at 1080×1920** in the brand's vertical layout for that format (e.g. graphics on top and face below, full face with captions, screen with a face bubble). Never just crop the 16:9 frame.
- Captions on, in the brand's caption spec, below the face (y 1200–1550 at 1080×1920) and clear of the platform buttons (`craft/layout.md`).
- The vertical motion row from the brand: shorter holds, shorter phrases.
- The series tag in the first 2–3 s, smaller. No end card; end on a beat that loops cleanly back to the start.
- Sting: cut or shortened to under 1 s, as the brand says.
- No watermarks from other apps.

## Platform notes (check live; rules change)

- **Safe zones:** draw each platform's current overlay (buttons, caption bar, profile row) over stills at gate 2. As a starting point, keep text out of roughly the bottom 20% and the right 15%.
- **Covers:** the brand's cover template, readable at grid size.
- **Instagram:** label AI-generated or altered content where required; Trial Reels can test a cut on non-followers; a "comment a keyword" call to action pairs well with an auto-reply.
- **Disclosure:** each platform's current rule for realistic synthetic media.

## Checks (part of gates 2–3)

- Phone-size contact sheet at 360 px wide: every word reads.
- Safe-zone overlay on every still with text.
- First frame check: is frame 1 already interesting with the sound off?
