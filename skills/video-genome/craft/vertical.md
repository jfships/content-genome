# Vertical: Shorts, Reels, TikTok

Shorts have their own track now: `shorts/README.md` (checklist), `shorts/build.md` (shooting, Take Check, cut, live preview, pace, layout, captions), `shorts/checks.md` (gates, timing numbers, technical review), `shorts/platforms.md` (files, covers, music, results), `shorts/shapes.md` (the six shapes).

Two rules from real failures:
- **A crop of the long edit is never a draft.** Long-video footage is only ever a part, rebuilt in a tall frame with a fresh hook.
- **The long video's order never transfers.** A Short is rebuilt in one shape.

The brand's vertical rules (layouts, caption and hook-text style, tall backdrop, endings, covers, music lane) come from the brand file; the 9:16 geometry is in `craft/layout.md` §1 and `shorts/build.md` "Layout".

## Instagram Stories (only when the brand has Stories rules)
Stories reach only people who already follow. Before making any Story, check the brand's Stories frame specs and follower gate in its vertical file; with none, or with the follower count under the gate (about 300), don't make Stories: make a Reel and say why in one line.
When made: 1080×1920; vertical video 5–15 s with the face or motion in the first second; native look (never a landscape thumbnail pasted on a color field); captions on; 3–4 frames in this order: hook clip, proof, poll, link frame last; one sticker per frame, on the last frame; a link sticker with custom promise text. Keep the top 250 px and bottom 340 px clear. Source and the full rules: brand-genome `catalog/instagram-carousels-stories.md`. Frames are built in the Shorts track like any tall piece; look choices with two or more real options go on a page (`decision-pages.md`).
