## 5. Look
### Palette
- Background #FAF6EC · words #222222 · pop color #FF5A36 · second color #2E86AB · pale #F0E6CF
### Fonts
- Titles: Permanent Marker · Text: Patrick Hand · Notes: Caveat (all in page-template/fonts)
### Color editing (footage)
- Main: warm paper: saturation 1.0, contrast 1.04, highlights toward #FAF6EC, light grain
### Text style
- Sticky Note (main) · Hand Annotation (backup) · Label Maker for names
### Captions
- Clean subtitle (main) · One word at a time for punchlines (backup)
### Backdrops

## 6. Movement
- Motion feel: Main Deadpan · Backup Glide
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entrance ease | `power3.out + steps(n) text` |
| Exit ease | `power2.in` |
| On-screen move | `power3.inOut` |
| Overshoot | 0 |
| Base unit | 0.4 s |
| Stagger | 60 ms |
| Transitions | Hard cut on the beat (default) · Match cut · Page Tear (gags only) (default listed first) |
| Hold | ≥ 0.6 s |
| Phrase length | long 8 beats / vertical 4 beats |
| Wildcard | Page Tear (at most one per video) |

## 7. Sound
| Lane | BPM | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Elevator bossa | 112 | deadpan bits |
| Mischief pizzicato | 120 | little disasters |
| Light acoustic | 105 | under talking |
| Lo-fi beats | 80 | calm parts |
- Sound effects: paper slap (hits) · pencil scribble (annotations) · record scratch (punchlines) · cartoon boing (fails, gags only). About 1 per 3 s at most, on transitions and reveals.
- No vocals under speech. Generated or licensed music only.

## 8. Kit
- Name tag: Lower third in the text font, pop-color bar, once, ~4 s, first time they speak.
- Series tag: open (only if they have a series).
- Sting: open. Default if none: the wordmark (their name in the title font) arriving with the kit's entrance ease, ~1 s, with the first sound effect listed above. After the hook, never before.
- Wordmark: their name in the title font, words color on background.
- End card: next video + their name, 5–8 s.
- Corner mark: off.
- Thumbnail layouts: Face + big words in marker (default) · Circled detail · Before / after. Words clear of the face and the bottom-right timestamp; title font, palette colors.

## 9. Vertical
- Layouts: Full frame + captions (default) · Handwritten notes over you · Before / after
- Motion: base units ×0.8; shots sized for their job, ceiling ~2.5 s (shorts/build.md "Pace"), speech at 2.6–3 words a second, punch-ins on the hook and punchline; no sting, sound logo, name tag or end card; end on the payoff or loop back to the start.
- Captions on, 2–3 word chunks, around y 1150–1400 below the face, clear of the platform buttons (craft/layout.md). Hook text 3–7 words from frame 1. Shot tall, never cropped from the long edit (shorts/build.md).
