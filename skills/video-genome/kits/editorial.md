## 5. Look
### Palette
- Background #F2EEE6 · words #1B1B1B · pop color #C8102E · second color #5B6770 · pale #E4DED2
### Fonts
- Titles: DM Serif Display · Text: Source Serif 4 · Notes: Instrument Serif (italic asides) (all in page-template/fonts)
### Color editing (footage)
- Main: soft film: saturation 0.92, contrast 1.03, lifted blacks, fine grain
### Text style
- Keynote Minimal (main) · Photo-Filled Type (backup) · Big Number for one stat
### Captions
- Clean subtitle (main) · Key words in the pop color (backup)
### Backdrops
- Light plain (default) · Paper · Real place blurred
### Extras kept
- Quote card · Lower third · Location card · Timeline scrub · Spotlight
### Signature moments
- Look Cascade · Progression Quad

## 6. Movement
- Motion feel: Main Drift · Backup Glide
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entrance ease | `sine.out` |
| Exit ease | `sine.in` |
| On-screen move | `sine.inOut` |
| Overshoot | 0 |
| Base unit | 0.8 s |
| Stagger | 120 ms |
| Transitions | Dissolve (default) · Match cut · Soft rise · J-cut / L-cut (default listed first) |
| Hold | ≥ 0.8 s |
| Phrase length | long 8 beats / vertical 4 beats |
| Wildcard | Tape Rewind (at most one per video) |

## 7. Sound
| Lane | BPM | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient | 70 | thoughtful parts |
| Jazz trio | 120 | under talking, late-night |
| Cinematic build | 96 | reveals |
| Light acoustic | 105 | lighter parts |
- Sound effects: paper turn (chapters) · soft chime (reveals) · camera shutter (photos). About 1 per 3 s at most, on transitions and reveals.
- No vocals under speech. Generated or licensed music only.

## 8. Kit
- Name tag: Lower third in the text font, pop-color bar, once, ~4 s, first time they speak.
- Series tag: open (only if they have a series).
- Sting: open. Default if none: the wordmark (their name in the title font) arriving with the kit's entrance ease, ~1 s, with the first sound effect listed above. After the hook, never before.
- Wordmark: their name in the title font, words color on background.
- End card: next video + their name, 5–8 s.
- Corner mark: off.
- Thumbnail layouts: Photo + one serif line (default) · Before / after. Words clear of the face and the bottom-right timestamp; title font, palette colors.

## 9. Vertical
- Layouts: Big quote (default) · Full frame + captions · You over a backdrop
- Motion: base units ×0.8; shots sized for their job, ceiling ~2.5 s (shorts/build.md "Pace"), speech at 2.6–3 words a second, punch-ins on the hook and punchline; no sting, sound logo, name tag or end card; end on the payoff or loop back to the start.
- Captions on, 2–3 word chunks, around y 1150–1400 below the face, clear of the platform buttons (craft/layout.md). Hook text 3–7 words from frame 1. Shot tall, never cropped from the long edit (shorts/build.md).
