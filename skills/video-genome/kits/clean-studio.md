## 5. Look
### Palette
- Background #F5F5F2 · words #121212 · pop color #2F6BFF · second color #9DB8FF · pale #E4E7EE
### Fonts
- Titles: Inter (800) · Text: Inter (400–600) · Notes: Caveat (all in page-template/fonts)
### Color editing (footage)
- Main: clean natural: saturation 1.03, contrast 1.05, no tint
### Text style
- Keynote Minimal (main) · Highlighter (backup) · Big Number for stats
### Captions
- Clean subtitle (main) · Highlight captions (backup); key word in the pop color
### Backdrops
- Light plain (default) · Soft gradient (chapter cards) · Your space, blurred
### Extras kept
- Callout · Checklist · Lower third · Progress steps · App window · Spotlight · Marker highlight
### Signature moments
- Freeze & Mark Up · Progression Quad

## 6. Movement
- Motion feel: Main Glide · Backup Snap
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entrance ease | `power2.out` |
| Exit ease | `power2.in` |
| On-screen move | `power2.inOut` |
| Overshoot | 0; scale 0.98 → 1 |
| Base unit | 0.5 s |
| Stagger | 80 ms |
| Transitions | Hard cut on the beat (default) · Soft rise · Dissolve · Match cut (default listed first) |
| Hold | ≥ 0.5 s |
| Phrase length | long 8 beats / vertical 4 beats |
| Wildcard | Crash Zoom (at most one per video) |

## 7. Sound
| Lane | BPM | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal electronic | 118 | under explaining |
| Light acoustic | 105 | under talking |
| Ambient | 70 | thoughtful parts |
| Cinematic build | 96 | reveals |
- Sound effects: soft click (UI, ticks) · air whoosh (moves) · soft chime (success). About 1 per 3 s at most, on transitions and reveals.
- No vocals under speech. Generated or licensed music only.

## 8. Kit
- Name tag: Lower third in the text font, pop-color bar, once, ~4 s, first time they speak.
- Series tag: open (only if they have a series).
- Sting: open. Default if none: the wordmark (their name in the title font) arriving with the kit's entrance ease, ~1 s, with the first sound effect listed above. After the hook, never before.
- Wordmark: their name in the title font, words color on background.
- End card: next video + their name, 5–8 s.
- Corner mark: off.
- Thumbnail layouts: Face + big words (default) · Screen + face · Before / after. Words clear of the face and the bottom-right timestamp; title font, palette colors.

## 9. Vertical
- Layouts: Full frame + captions (default) · Screen on top, you below · Big quote
- Motion: base units ×0.8; a change every 3–5 s, punch-ins on the hook and punchline; no sting, sound logo, name tag or end card; end on the payoff or loop back to the start.
- Captions on, 2–3 word chunks, around y 1150–1400 below the face, clear of the platform buttons (craft/layout.md). Hook text 3–7 words from frame 1. Shot tall, never cropped from the long edit (shorts/build.md).
