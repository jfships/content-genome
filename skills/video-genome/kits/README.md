# Included kits

Eight complete Brand Sheets for creators with no brand kit (`setup/brand-intake.md`). Each uses the Brand Genome headings, so everything in video-genome reads it the same way. Fonts are all in `page-template/fonts/`; moves are named as in `craft/graphics-library.md` where it has them (others are built per "Building catalog options" there); motion rows match `craft/motion.md`.

| Kit | For |
|---|---|
| `clean-studio.md` · Clean studio | calm, clear explainers and tutorials |
| `night-desk.md` · Night desk | tech, tools, screens and builds |
| `marker-paper.md` · Marker and paper | handmade, funny, honest takes |
| `bold-block.md` · Bold block | high energy, reactions, big claims |
| `editorial.md` · Editorial | stories, essays, slower and premium |
| `arcade.md` · Arcade | games, internet culture, playful |
| `calm-natural.md` · Calm natural | lifestyle, food, slow living, wellness |
| `soup-kitchen.md` · Soup Kitchen | food spots, small businesses, funny day-in-the-life, made-up clay characters |

Shown on the included-kits page (`page-template/examples/brand-kits.json`, seed list `KITS` in `gen_examples.py`; keep both in step with these files). Picked kit → copied to `Brand Sheet.md` with `kit:` and `backup kit:` lines at the top. Sections marked `open` are filled from the creator the first time a video needs them.

## Names used in the kits

- **Art styles:** Isometric (a tidy 3D-from-above diagram world) · Paper diorama (cut-paper layers like a shoebox stage) · Low-poly (faceted simple 3D) · Neon cyberpunk (night city, glowing signs) · Pencil (sketchbook drawing) · Xerox zine (photocopied, high-contrast collage) · Pop comic (halftone comic panel) · Manga (black-and-white Japanese comic) · Engraving (old banknote line art) · Watercolor · Gouache (flat opaque paint) · Museum (the scene as a framed exhibit) · Voxel (3D pixel blocks) · Synthwave (80s grid sunset) · Claymation.
- **Cutaway gags:** Nature documentary (a hushed narrator describes the creator like wildlife) · Weather report (a forecast for a trend) · Boss fight (a problem as a video-game boss) · Breaking news (something small announced as huge) · Courtroom (a habit or advice put on trial) · Infomercial ("Are YOU tired of…?") · Confessional (reality-show aside to camera) · Award show (a trophy for a tiny win) · Sports commentary (two commentators call the moment) · Trailer (an ordinary task cut like a movie trailer) · Tiny me (a miniature creator giving directions) · Game show (a quiz or big choice).
- **Extras not in the graphics library:** Sticker (a die-cut sticker slapped on the frame) · Countdown (a big number counting down to a reveal) · Quote card (one line set large with who said it).
- **Vertical layouts:** Full frame + captions · Screen on top, you below · Cut-out over the screen · Big quote · Number behind you · Reaction (you small, the thing you react to big) · Floating head (your face in a circle over a screen) · Countdown list · Handwritten notes over you · Before / after · You over a backdrop.
