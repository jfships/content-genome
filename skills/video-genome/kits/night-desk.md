## 6. Movement
- Motion feel: Main Snap · Backup Mechanical
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entrance ease | `expo.out` |
| Exit ease | `power2.in` |
| On-screen move | `power3.inOut` |
| Overshoot | 0 |
| Base unit | 0.35 s |
| Stagger | 50 ms |
| Transitions | Hard cut on the beat (default) · Split slide · Zoom through (default listed first) |
| Hold | ≥ 0.3 s |
| Phrase length | long 8 beats / vertical 4 beats |
| Wildcard | Pixel Collapse (at most one per video) |

## 7. Sound
| Lane | BPM | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal electronic | 118 | under explaining |
| Synthwave | 100 | intros, night |
| Tension pulse | 128 | before a reveal |
| Lo-fi beats | 80 | under long screen work |
- Sound effects: mech keys (typing) · ui click (cursor) · synth riser (reveals) · error beep (fails). About 1 per 3 s at most, on transitions and reveals.
- No vocals under speech. Generated or licensed music only.

## 8. Kit
- Name tag: Lower third in the text font, pop-color bar, once, ~4 s, first time they speak.
- Series tag: open (only if they have a series).
- Sting: open. Default if none: the wordmark (their name in the title font) arriving with the kit's entrance ease, ~1 s, with the first sound effect listed above. After the hook, never before.
- Wordmark: their name in the title font, words color on background.
- End card: next video + their name, 5–8 s.
- Corner mark: off.
- Thumbnail layouts: Screen + face (default) · Versus · Circled detail. Words clear of the face and the bottom-right timestamp; title font, palette colors.

## 9. Vertical
- Layouts: Screen on top, you below (default) · Cut-out over the screen · Full frame + captions
- Motion: base units ×0.8; shots sized for their job, ceiling ~2.5 s (shorts/build.md "Pace"), speech at 2.6–3 words a second, punch-ins on the hook and punchline; no sting, sound logo, name tag or end card; end on the payoff or loop back to the start.
- Captions on, 2–3 word chunks, around y 1150–1400 below the face, clear of the platform buttons (craft/layout.md). Hook text 3–7 words from frame 1. Shot tall, never cropped from the long edit (shorts/build.md).
