## 5. Look
### Palette
- Background #111111 · words #FFFFFF · pop color #FFDD00 · second color #FF3B30 · pale #2A2A2A
### Fonts
- Titles: Anton · Text: Inter (600–800) · Notes: Permanent Marker (all in page-template/fonts)
### Color editing (footage)
- Main: punchy: saturation 1.12, contrast 1.15, slight vignette
### Text style
- Kinetic Bold (main) · Big Number (backup) · Stencil for chapter titles
### Captions
- One word at a time (main) · Highlight captions (backup); highlight in the pop color
### Backdrops
- Dark plain (default) · Pop color behind you · Moving type wall
### Extras kept
- Big Number · Stat bubble · Word pop · Text behind speaker · Countdown · Spotlight
### Signature moments
- Word Burst · Clone Grid

## 6. Movement
- Motion feel: Main Punch · Backup Snap
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entrance ease | `expo.out` |
| Exit ease | `power3.in` |
| On-screen move | `power4.inOut` |
| Overshoot | 0–small |
| Base unit | 0.3 s |
| Stagger | 40 ms |
| Transitions | Cut on action (default) · Crash zoom · Whip pan (default listed first) |
| Hold | ≥ 0.25 s |
| Phrase length | long 8 beats / vertical 4 beats |
| Wildcard | Slot Machine (at most one per video) |

## 7. Sound
| Lane | BPM | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Boom bap | 90 | intros, swagger |
| Indie pop | 115 | montages |
| Tension pulse | 128 | before a reveal |
| Tropical house | 108 | wins |
- Sound effects: deep thud (big type) · air whoosh (moves) · bass drop (punchlines) · cash register (wins). About 1 per 3 s at most, on transitions and reveals.
- No vocals under speech. Generated or licensed music only.

## 8. Kit
- Name tag: Lower third in the text font, pop-color bar, once, ~4 s, first time they speak.
- Series tag: open (only if they have a series).
- Sting: open. Default if none: the wordmark (their name in the title font) arriving with the kit's entrance ease, ~1 s, with the first sound effect listed above. After the hook, never before.
- Wordmark: their name in the title font, words color on background.
- End card: next video + their name, 5–8 s.
- Corner mark: off.
- Thumbnail layouts: One big word (default) · Face + big words · Versus. Words clear of the face and the bottom-right timestamp; title font, palette colors.

## 9. Vertical
- Layouts: Full frame + captions (default) · Number behind you · Reaction
- Motion: base units ×0.8; a change every 3–5 s, punch-ins on the hook and punchline; no sting, sound logo, name tag or end card; end on the payoff or loop back to the start.
- Captions on, 2–3 word chunks, around y 1150–1400 below the face, clear of the platform buttons (craft/layout.md). Hook text 3–7 words from frame 1. Shot tall, never cropped from the long edit (shorts/build.md).
