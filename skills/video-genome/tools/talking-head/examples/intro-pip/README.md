# Worked example: an 84-second profile intro (personal / trust tone)

The speaker fills the frame and sits right of centre, in warm, dim footage. So: dark brand canvas, no side panels, and every
framing change re-centres on him (focus:'auto').

The final cut (plan.json) was agreed with the user in the interview:
- Speaker to side for the job titles (08), the kitchen backstory (02) and the disconnected-tools story (09). His face never leaves.
- One full cutaway for the proof moment (04).
- One word pop tucked behind him (06). Laid out from `subject.py box` so only word edges pass behind the head, and verified with `subject.py occlusion` (≥96% visible).
- Soft background blur, 3 gentle zoom-ins with focus "auto", plain captions (hidden during the side clips), and a lower-third invite at the close (05).

Also in the folder: 01 and 03 are the full-cutaway versions of the same ideas (first cut), and 07 shows the real-screenshot look with a stand-in image.
Clip times are clip-local. The comment at the top of each script lists the word times each clip was built on.
