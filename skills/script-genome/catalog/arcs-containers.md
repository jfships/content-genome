# Arcs: containers

Family: a frame of slots that holds another arc or a journey; a container says where the turns go, not how the feeling moves, so name the arc that runs inside it.
Shared skeleton: `[setup + question or mission] [attempt, but …] [midpoint change] [harder attempt] [answer + so-what]`.
Evidence: no arc tested at 30–60 s; act proportions come from craft (Aristotle, Freytag, screenwriting beat sheets) and uncited vendor splits for short video; the vendors agree only on hook in ~1.5–3 s, a turn near the middle, payoff at the end. Full hero's journeys, falling action and subplots do not compress (each beat falls under 5 s).
Runtimes: three-acts 30–60 s; day-in-the-life 30–60 s; hero-short ≥ 45 s; quest ≥ 45 s at full length (a 3-beat version fits 30 s).

Card fields as in `arcs-setback.md`. Words per beat ≈ seconds × 2.4. All examples are made up.

### three-acts · Three acts
Type: CONTAINER
Does: setup with the question stated, a first attempt, a midpoint that flips the plan, a harder attempt, the answer.
Use when: story, demo, comparison. 30–60 s. Say which arc runs inside Act 2 (e.g. man-in-hole, fail-fail-win).
Beats: ACT 1 20% (0–6 | 0–12) hook + question stated by ~12 s at 60 s · ACT 2A 30% (6–15 | 12–30) · ACT 2B 25% (15–22.5 | 30–45) after the midpoint flip · ACT 3 25% (22.5–30 | 45–60). Plot points at 20 / 50 / 75%.
V: [0, -1, -2, +2] `▄▂▁█`
Lines: connective at each plot point: `but` (end of act 1), a reversal line (midpoint), `until` / `so` (end of act 2). `[who/what/want + question] [first attempt, but …] [midpoint: new info flips the plan] [so <harder attempt>] [answer + so-what]`
  e.g. "I wanted to fix a squeaky floor without lifting a board." / "Halfway in, I found the squeak wasn't the board at all. It was the joist under it."
Edit: act boundaries are edit boundaries: change framing or cut rate at 20% and 75% · the midpoint at 50% gets the one INT · act 3 under ~15 words is a weak ending; give it room.
Pairs: any hook that states the question (question, stakes, callout); endings any default. Clashes: none native.
Fails when: used as the arc (it says nothing about feeling); act 1 runs past 25%; no midpoint change.
Evidence: CRAFT/MANY [5] (Aristotle, Freytag, screenwriting beat sheet) + VENDOR short-form splits. No research on act proportions at any length.

### hero-short · Hero's journey, short
Type: CONTAINER
Does: comfort and a want, crossing into something unfamiliar, trials, a prize at a cost, a return changed.
Use when: story, origin. ≥ 45 s only (5-beat compression of an 8-step circle).
Beats: B1 COMFORT + WANT 15% (n/a | 0–9) hook · B2 THRESHOLD 15% (n/a | 9–18) · B3 TRIALS 32% (n/a | 18–37) · B4 PRIZE AT A COST 20% (n/a | 37–49) · B5 RETURN CHANGED 18% (n/a | 49–60). At 45 s: 0–6.75 | 6.75–13.5 | 13.5–28 | 28–37 | 37–45.
V: [0, -1, -1, +2, +1] `▄▂▂█▆`
Lines: the inner change in one line at B5 ("I used to think <x>, now <y>"); the cost in B4 is what makes it feel earned. `[normal + want] [so I tried <unfamiliar>] [I learned <x>, <y>, but <trial>] [I got <prize>, at the cost of <cost>] [back at <place>, now I <changed behavior>]`
  e.g. "I wanted to run a marathon without ever joining a club." / "I finished. But I lost the thing that kept me running: people to run with."
Edit: B2 hard cut to a new place or setup (the threshold is visible) · B3 CUT-F montage, STEP · B4 SIL at the cost line · B5 return to the B1 place and framing, MATCH to the first shot.
Pairs: hooks pov-setup, stakes, mid-action; operator full-circle; endings loop, callback; tease-next stage-gated. Clashes: hard asks.
Fails when: under 45 s; "changed" is a generic moral; no real cost.
Evidence: CRAFT [1], one lineage (Campbell, story circle). No short-form evidence. Flag: weak

### quest · Quest / mission
Type: CONTAINER · Aliases: mission
Does: a yes/no mission with stakes, an obstacle, a tool or step that guides, a final test, the verdict.
Use when: demo, test. 45–60 s at full length; at 30 s run mission, obstacle + fix, verdict (3 beats).
Beats: B1 MISSION 14% (0–4 | 0–8.5) hook · B2 OBSTACLE 26% (4–12 | 8.5–24) · B3 GUIDE/TOOL 15% (12–16.5 | 24–33) · B4 FINAL TEST 30% (16.5–25.5 | 33–51) · B5 OUTCOME 15% (25.5–30 | 51–60). @30: B2+B3 one beat; B5 the last line of B4.
V: [0, -1, +1, -1, +2] `▄▂▆▂█`
Lines: the mission is one line with a yes/no outcome ("Can I …?"); the tool or step is the guide that gives a plan. `[can I <big thing> with <constraint>?] [first problem: <x>] [then I found <tool/step>] [final test: <y>] [verdict: yes/no + how]`
  e.g. (made-up) "Can I make a week of dinners for two from one $30 shop?" / "First problem: by Wednesday the herbs were dead. Then I froze them in oil."
Edit: chapter labels (STEP "Level 1/2/3"), CUT-F, a progress bar if the brand has one · final test gets TICK or RISE, INT @test · outcome CARD-N.
Pairs: hooks question, stakes, number; endings payoff-stop, send-line; tease-next stage-gated. Clashes: asks aimed at people who already follow (callout CTAs).
Fails when: the mission is vague; no real obstacle; the outcome is "it kind of worked".
Evidence: CRAFT [2] (StoryBrand-style plan, vendor; story circle). Flag: weak

### day-in-the-life · Day in the life
Type: ORDER (time stamps) · used as a container
Does: time-stamped scenes with a hidden through-line, paying off at one moment where something breaks or works.
Use when: story, demo (a workflow). 30–60 s. Needs b-roll; not talking head only.
Beats: B1 HOOK 8% (0–2.5 | 0–5) names the payoff moment · B2 SCENES 64% (2.5–21.5 | 5–43) 4 scenes ≈ 5 s at 30; 6–8 ≈ 6 s at 60 · B3 PAYOFF MOMENT 20% (21.5–27.5 | 43–55) · B4 SIGN-OFF 8% (27.5–30 | 55–60) last line of B3 at 30 s.
V: [0, 0, 0, +2, +1] `▄▄▄█▆`
Lines: a time stamp as the first words of each scene ("7:40 a.m."); a through-line question set in the hook ("what breaks at 3 p.m.?"). `[a day running <x>; the 3 p.m. part saved me] [<time>: <scene> × N] [<time>: the moment <x> happened] [sign-off]`
  e.g. "7:40 a.m. Open the shop, check yesterday's orders." / "3:15 p.m. The card reader dies, and one backup fixes it."
Edit: STEP clock stamps, CUT-M, BEAT cuts, music constant at MUS-LOW · payoff scene: longer hold, CARD-N, INT @moment.
Pairs: hooks pov-setup, curiosity-gap; endings loop, payoff-stop; tease-next stage-gated. Clashes: warning, stakes.
Fails when: scenes are pure routine; no payoff moment; talking head with no b-roll.
Evidence: CRAFT (vlog tradition) + INFERENCE [1]. Flag: weak
