# Awareness read, symptom-first opener, rewrite pass, pro read

Used by job A (worksheet line 12, outline order, pass f, pass l) and job B (report section B2). The commonest gap between a competent script and a professional one is not the shape: it is wording pitched at a viewer who knows more (or less) than the real viewer does. This file names the viewer's level, tests the opener against it, rewrites the lines that miss, then reads the whole script once the way a paid script editor would.

Basis: the five awareness levels come from direct-response copywriting (Eugene Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*, 1966) and are standard practitioner craft (MANY); symptom-first openers are the same idea applied to the first line. Neither has been tested on 20–60 s Reels. Flag: weak. Never a gate: findings are craft notes S14–S16 with rewrites.

## 1. Name the level (card header `awareness: <level> · <source>`)
Source, in order: the Idea Record's audience line → the growth plan's `audience` and `post_job_now` → ask the creator (one question). A post meant to reach strangers defaults to unaware or problem-aware. At stage "no audience yet", never most aware.

| Level | What the viewer already has | Opener speaks to | Name the method or product? | Default order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | a symptom they live with, no name for it | the symptom, in a scene from their day, in their words | no, not before the turn | symptom → cause → fix → proof |
| Problem-aware | the problem's name, no idea a fix exists | the symptom, then what it costs them | the problem yes; the fix after the cost | symptom → cost → fix → proof |
| Solution-aware | knows fixes exist, not this one | the result, or why the usual fix fails | the method by beat 2 | result → why the usual fix fails → how this works → proof |
| Product-aware | knows the creator's thing, not convinced | proof, the difference, the objection | yes | proof → difference → ask |
| Most aware | ready, waiting for a reason | the offer, plainly | yes | offer → one proof → ask |

## 2. Symptom-first opener (default in every script)
The first spoken line and the hook screen text name what the viewer feels or sees in their own day (the symptom), not the problem's label and not the fix. The script then resolves the symptom by resolving the problem under it.
- Test: could the viewer say this line about their own week, word for word? If they'd need the creator's vocabulary to say it, it's a label.
- Invented examples. Label: "Your pricing strategy is broken." Symptom: "Fully booked all month, and the account still looks the same." Label: "You need a scheduling system." Symptom: "Your phone buzzed forty times while you were with a client." Label: "Most freelancers undercharge." Symptom: "You finished the project and felt worse than when you started."
- A result-first hook ("I cut my biggest bill by forty percent") passes when the result removes a symptom the viewer feels and that symptom is said or shown by ~5 s. Otherwise it's solution-aware wording: fine for a solution-aware post, a rewrite for anything lower.
- Breaking the default (product-aware or most-aware posts, a deliberate label-first contrarian line) needs a reason written in the card (belief 1).
- Symptom lines come from the creator's raw words, the brand's owner-pain or audience research and its hook bank first; a symptom the audience never said is `new` and needs a source or the creator's yes.

## 3. Rewrite pass (job A pass l; job B section B2)
For each beat, check four things and rewrite only what misses:
1. **Words:** a label, tool name or insider term a viewer at this level wouldn't use → swap for the words they'd use (C5 glosses the rest).
2. **Assumed knowledge:** the line needs something this level doesn't have yet → add the missing step or cut the line.
3. **Order:** the beats follow the level's default order (table above), or the card says why not.
4. **Ask:** the ending's ask fits the level. Unaware and problem-aware: payoff-stop, save or a situational send line; a DM or offer ask from a viewer who just learned they have a problem is a stretch (note it, the growth plan still decides).
Output: `beat | "line" | misses (words / knowledge / order / ask) | rewrite (src new)`. Keep-voice rules apply: the creator vetoes any rewrite; a signature line reverts by default.

## 4. Three-level test (optional)
When the level is unclear, or to learn which level this audience answers to: rewrite only the hook and beat 1 for three adjacent levels, offer them as hook alternatives, and log the level that posted (`learn/log.md`, one variable at a time).

## 5. Pro read (once, before lock or at the end of a check)
Read top to bottom as a paid script editor. Each answer is a quoted line or "missing"; a "missing" gets a fix or a question to the creator, never an invented fact.
1. **One viewer:** can you picture one person, in one moment of their day, this is for?
2. **Their words:** at least one line the viewer would say themselves.
3. **Cost of scrolling:** by ~5 s, what does the viewer lose by leaving?
4. **Earned turn:** does the middle change what the viewer believed at the start, or only add facts?
5. **Only-you line:** one line no other creator could say (their proof, a detail from their life).
6. **Every line earns its seconds:** name any line whose cut the viewer wouldn't notice.
