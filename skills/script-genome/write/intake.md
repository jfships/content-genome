# Intake and one idea (job A, steps 1–2)

## Intake: take the raw words as they are
Accept: typed notes, a voice-memo transcript, a long-video transcript, a list of tips, a rough script. Keep the raw text verbatim in the working file; don't clean it yet. If audio length is known, measure the creator's pace: words ÷ seconds (use it instead of the default in `budget.md`). The best voice source is the creator talking the idea through out loud; if there's only typed text, ask once for a 60–90 s voice memo "explaining it to a friend" (optional, recommended).

Words source: tag every raw line `take` (recorded take), `memo` (voice memo), `chat` (typed in chat or notes) or later `new` (Claude's). Performed lines (`take`, `memo`) are the wording source. Typed `chat` words are content, not wording: they stay marked `chat` in the card until the creator says them aloud. If the memo lines are weak or missing for a beat, say so and ask for a short new voice memo on those beats instead of polishing weak lines.

Ask only what's missing, at most 3 (AskUserQuestion, Recommended first):
1. Who is this for (one line, specific: "people who edit on their phone", not "creators")?
2. What should they know or be able to do after?
3. What's the proof: did you try it, is there a screen recording, a real number?

## Worksheet (fill from the raw words; save in the working file)
```
RAW: <verbatim, each line tagged src: take | memo | chat>
1 CORE CLAIM (≤20 words): [Audience] can [outcome] by [method] because [proof/reason].
2 AUDIENCE (who; what they already know):
3 PROOF (what the creator actually did/saw/measured; date; tool or product version):
4 PAYOFF (what the viewer can do/know by the end):
5 ONE NEXT STEP (or none):
6 IDEAS FOUND: id | one line | proof Y/N | fits budget Y/N | needs prior context Y/N
7 SIGNATURE LINES (verbatim phrases, openers, jokes, idioms worth keeping):
8 PERISHABLE FACTS (names, prices, limits, dates): verify before recording
9 CONNECTIONS TO DISCLOSE (paid, free product/credits, affiliate, equity, friend/family):
10 MADE WITH (AI voice, avatar, generated footage, if any):
11 STAGE (from the growth plan (`## Growth state`), or "no audience yet"):
```

## One idea per video: tests
Source-backed principle: one idea per short video; split bigger topics into a series. The tests are judgment aids:
- **A, the "and" test.** State the takeaway in one sentence with no "and". Needs "and" → two videos.
- **B, promise = payoff.** Does the opening promise the same thing the last beat pays off? If not, split or rewrite one end.
- **C, delete a beat.** If a beat can go and the rest is still a whole video, the deleted beat is video two.
- **D, budget.** After cutting, words over the runtime's budget (`budget.md`) → it's a longer video or two short ones.
- **E, two audiences or two asks** → two videos.
- **F, stranger test.** A stranger who never saw the creator gets the point from this video alone. Fail → reject or rebuild, don't rescue.
Leftover ideas go to a **parked list** in the working file (and to the idea bank if idea-genome is in use), never added as "bonus tips".

## Converting a list of tips
Don't turn 5 tips into 5 equal beats (≈26 words each at 60 s, no room for proof). Default: pick the ONE tip with first-hand proof, park the rest (or plan a series). If the list is the point: 3 items max under 45 s, ≥50% of words on the best one, each item labeled on screen (`STEP`), the ranking criterion stated, strongest item first or last (never buried in the middle). If the hook says "3", the script delivers 3.

## Converting a long video or transcript
Find a self-contained passage: claim + proof + payoff that makes sense with zero prior context (test: pretend the long video doesn't exist). Lift the creator's own words; reorder so the point leads; cut setup and asides; re-record the hook fresh for vertical. A crop of a long edit is never a draft. Re-editing your own long video is original; someone else's clip with subtitles or credit only is not.

## Rough script handed in
Treat it as raw words: extract the claim first, then compare. Keep its structure if it passes the function read (`check/report.md` B); change only what fails.
