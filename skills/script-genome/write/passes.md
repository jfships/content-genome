# Draft and passes (job A, steps 5–6)

## Draft from the creator's words
- Assemble each beat from the creator's own phrases first (worksheet line 7, the raw text, the line ledger). Fill gaps with the fewest connecting words.
- Every card line carries `src: take | memo | chat | new`. `take` / `memo` = words the creator performed (a recorded take, a voice memo): these are the wording source. `chat` = words the creator typed: content, not wording; the creator says them aloud before they're kept. `new` = a line Claude adds, so the creator keeps or cuts it.
- If the memo lines for a beat are weak or missing, say so and ask for a short new voice memo on those beats (name the beats and what each must say) instead of polishing weak lines.
- **Never add** experiences, opinions, feelings, results, numbers or quotes the creator didn't give. (Also a legal line: don't describe an experience with a product that didn't happen.)
- Keep verbatim: signature phrases, running jokes, openers, nicknames for tools, regional words, their usual sentence length. Edit only to remove false starts and fillers, reorder, cut, fix facts, add specifics they gave.
- Voice calibration when available: 2–3 past videos they like → average words per sentence, contraction habit, favorite openers.

## Passes (in this order; each pass changes one thing)
| Pass | What it does | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| a. Cut | Remove everything not serving the core claim. Cut at least ~10% of the first draft; keep cutting until it fits `budget.md`; stop before the proof beat dies. ("Cut 10%" is a writer's rule of thumb, not research.) | Spoken words ≤ budget +10%; the timed read-aloud (pass i) decides |
| b. Specifics | Every beat holds at least one of: a number, a named tool/product/place, a named button or screen, an action on screen, a time, a before/after. No beat made only of abstractions ("workflow", "streamline", "leverage"). Use real figures ("22 days", "$2,700"), not rounded vagueness. | Each beat has a concrete anchor |
| c. Show | For any beat where the picture is the creator's face for >5 s, or the feeling is flat: propose a device or joke from the creator's own material (memo, line ledger, past videos), a graphic with a job (video-genome `craft/graphic-jobs.md` if installed; otherwise pick one: straight man, unsaid line, exaggeration, proof, breather), or ask the creator for one. Never invent a fact. | Every face-only run >5 s and every flat beat has a proposal or a question to the creator (K16) |
| d. Verbs | Active verbs over "is/has/provides + noun"; hunt nominalisations ("provides editing assistance" → "helps you edit"). | No noun-stack sentences |
| e. Hedges and tells | Cut stacked weak hedges (kinda, maybe, sort of, I guess); keep ONE calibrated first-person qualifier when a claim is genuinely uncertain ("in my tests", "as of this week"). Run `voice/tells.md` (flag clusters only). | No mush, no false confidence |
| f. Hook | First ≤8 spoken words carry the point; the three layers are written (spoken / screen text 3–7 words, offered in both versions: own words and mirror (`catalog/hooks.md`) / first image); the hook's promise matches the payoff. Check the hook card's `Fails when`. | Three layers filled, promise traced to a later line |
| g. Ending | Last line is a payoff, button, hold or loop (`catalog/endings.md`); any ask is allowed at this stage (growth plan); no outro. | Ending card named |
| h. Facts | Every name, price, limit, date and number has a source and date; perishable ones say "as of <month>" (spoken or on screen). Anything unverified is flagged, not stated. | Fact list with sources |
| i. Read aloud and time | The creator reads it at natural pace with a timer; mark stumbles and breath breaks; split long sentences; add pause marks (`voice/delivery.md`); re-time. | Fits runtime in one natural take |
| j. Keep-voice diff | Show before/after for every changed line. The creator can veto any line; a rewritten signature line reverts by default. | Creator has seen the diff |
| k. Whole script (before lock) | Read it top to bottom as a viewer who doesn't know the creator, not line by line. List the jobs and quote the line doing each: **hook** (why stop), **proof** (why believe), **why / stakes** (why is this person telling me this, and why should I care: what they lost, risked, or will save me), **turn** (where it changes direction or reveals), **payoff** (the hook's promise kept), **loop** (only if the ending loops: the last line that leads back into the first). A job with no line is a gap: write the line from the creator's own words, or ask them for it; never lock with a gap. A job that genuinely doesn't apply says n.a. and why ("no turn: a straight list"). Then re-time if a line was added. | Every job has a quoted line or a stated n.a.; shown to the creator with the card |

## Output of job A
1. The script card (`card/schema.md`), lint run (`card/lint.md`), and the job list from pass k.
2. Before/after list.
3. Parked ideas.
4. Facts to verify, with who confirms.
5. Anything invented: must be empty.
