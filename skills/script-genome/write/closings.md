# Closings and asks (job A, step 3 parts and pass g)

Use when writing the last beat. Many creators feel least at ease with their closings and have no defined asks; this step gives them 2–3 closes to choose from, each in their own register, and keeps the ones they keep so a defined set builds up over time.

## 1. Read (only what exists)
1. The growth plan's `## Growth state`: `cta_allowed_now`, `cta_not_yet`, `house_bans`, and the slot's job in the Idea Record (`catalog/endings.md` §4). No growth plan → the default stage (SKILL.md).
2. The brand's **kept closings**: lines tagged `close` in `00 Decisions/line-ledger.md` (kept and cut, with reasons). These are the creator's defined closes; offer one first when it fits.
3. The brand's **closings bank** (`04 Ideas/swipe-library.md`, section "Closings bank"): closes seen in saves, with what the creator tapped. Liked ones first; ones they passed on never come back as a pick.
4. The brand's **CTA bank** (`04 Ideas/cta-bank.md`): fill-in ask lines grouped by goal (soft sell, share, conversation, save, community). Use only groups whose ask is in `cta_allowed_now`.

## 2. Offer 2–3 closes
- **Always one no-ask close**: payoff-stop, button, deadpan hold, callback or a loop (`catalog/endings.md` §1). It is the default when the creator is unsure.
- **One from the creator's own kept or liked closes**, if any fit the video.
- **One ask, matched to the post's job**: share → a situational send line; save → a save prompt with the moment they'll need it; follow → a follow with a reason this video earned; conversation → one real opinion question; lead → the softest allowed offer line.
- Fill every bracket with this video's own noun ("send this to the friend who still answers the shop phone at 9 pm", invented), never a generic frame. A bank line is a shape: rewrite it in the creator's voice; the line is `new` until they say it aloud.
- Each close carries one line on why it fits and which ending card it is. The ask, if any, lands after the payoff or at a mid-video peak, never instead of the payoff (`catalog/endings.md` §4.4).
- Gate checks still apply: L9 (no bait), L11 (allowed at this stage), C6 (one ask or none).

## 3. After the lock
Write the close the creator kept to the line ledger's kept list, with `close` in the note (line · video · ending card · why kept). The closes they cut go to its cut list the same way, with their reason, so they don't come back. After a few videos, the kept list is the brand's defined set of closes.
