# Write from saved devices (job A, worked backwards)

Use when the creator asks for a script built from the script devices they saved ("pick from what we've saved and work backwards"), on a topic they give or on none. Devices set the order and each line's job; the facts, proof and wording still come from the creator (belief 3).

1. **Gather the bank.** Read the brand's `04 Ideas/swipe-library.md`: its script-tools bank, its closings bank, and entries marked CRAFT. List each device once: name · source (swipe number or catalog slug) · the job it does (hook, re-hook, turn, proof, ending) · the creator's note. Count only devices the creator flagged; an untapped item in a save they liked is a maybe (swipe-file rule). No swipe library: use the catalog cards alone and say so.
2. **Map each device to a card**: hook slug (`hooks*.md`), retention slug (`retention.md`), shape (`shapes.md`), ending slug (`endings.md`). A device with no card keeps the creator's wording of it.
3. **Topic.** Given: go on. None: propose 3 topics the creator can back with proof (`Who I Am.md` Proof and Takes they hold, the idea bank, an open slot in the growth plan), each with one line and the devices it fits best. The creator picks (AskUserQuestion).
4. **Build 2 stacks, from the end back to the start**: ending → payoff → re-hook → turn → hook. Each stack: 3–5 devices, one per job, held by one shape that passes the selector's five checks (`catalog/selector.md` step 5). Offer both with why each fits the topic; the creator picks.
5. **Then the normal flow from step 1** (`SKILL.md` job A) with the stack as the beat plan: intake for the picked topic (ask for a voice memo on it), outline, draft, passes, card. Every device line is `new` until the creator says it aloud. At step 7, list the devices used with their sources beside the before/after list.

Done when: the card locks with every beat traced to a device or to the creator's own words, and every device to its swipe or catalog source.
